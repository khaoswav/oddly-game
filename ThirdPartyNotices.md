# Third-party notices and asset register

No third-party game code, art, music, voice recordings or sound effects are bundled in the initial prototype.

The current greybox uses Unity built-in primitives, standard engine components and editor-generated UI. Unity is a separate third-party product and its license terms apply to use of the engine.

Before adding any external asset, record:
- Asset name and creator.
- Original source URL.
- Exact license and a saved copy/snapshot of its terms.
- Whether commercial use, modification and redistribution are permitted.
- Required attribution.
- Files/locations where the asset is used.

Do not ship assets marked personal-use-only, non-commercial, or with unclear redistribution rights. Prefer original commissioned work or licenses explicitly compatible with commercial game distribution.
